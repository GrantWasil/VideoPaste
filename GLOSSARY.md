# VideoPaste

VideoPaste copies a video from a Reddit or X post to the clipboard, from the macOS app or from a button the browser extensions place on the video.

## Language

**Post link**:
The address of a Reddit or X post page.
_Avoid_: Post URL, permalink, source URL

**Media URL**:
An address that leads to the video itself, with no post page involved.
_Avoid_: Direct URL, video address, source URL

**Manifest URL**:
A media URL for a playlist that lists a video's renditions and audio, in HLS or DASH form.
_Avoid_: Playlist link, stream URL
